🧠 What is Brain Dumpz?

Brain Dumpz is a simple organization tool designed to help you get thoughts out of your head instantly.

Instead of trying to organize everything before writing it down, you can simply dump your thoughts into the app. Brain Dumpz then helps organize them into categories, making it easier to see what you need to do, remember, plan, or come back to later.



The idea is simple:

Think it → Dump it → Organize it → Get it done.

📱 How To Use Brain Dumpz
-Open Brain Dumpz
-Start by typing whatever is on your mind into the Brain Dump area. Don't worry about making it perfect.
-Dump Your Thoughts
-Write down tasks, ideas, reminders, plans, random thoughts, or anything else you don't want to forget.
-Let Brain Dumpz Organize It
-The app analyzes your text and sorts your thoughts into useful categories.
-Check "My Tasks"
-Tasks that need your attention can be viewed in the My Tasks section so you can keep track of what needs to get done.
-Use "More" For Everything Else
-Other organized thoughts can be found through the More section and its categories.
-Save Your Brain Dump
-Save your organized thoughts so you can come back to them later instead of having to rewrite everything.
-Keep Dumping
-Whenever your brain gets crowded again, come back and dump it. You don't need to organize your thoughts before using Brain Dumpz.
